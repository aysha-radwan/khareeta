Privacy Policy (English)

What this is. A gift page: the course “Khareetat Al-Kawn” is offered free to followers of @alkoun_. When you comment the word «خريطة» on one of my Instagram posts, the page link is sent to you automatically as a private reply.

What we read. Only comments on @alkoun_’s own posts, to detect the keyword. We do not read your direct messages and we never ask for an email, phone number or any personal information.

What we store. The ID of the comment that was answered and the username, solely so the link is not sent twice. Nothing else is stored, nothing is shared with third parties, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Deletion. Message @alkoun_ or email cocreate@alkoun.org and the record is removed within days.
